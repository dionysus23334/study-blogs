Re-roll after the shot

Gloam Cam Yingze Fan (independent developer) 2026-11-26

The hardest part of film photography is choosing the film before you press the shutter. The app in today's report — a film-simulation camera that reached Taiwan's overall top 5 free chart — keeps a 'digital negative' for every shot, so you can change the film, tone and white balance afterwards, and clear the negative when you're done. This demo does just that one thing: shoot first, re-roll in the darkroom later. The scenes, the 'Dusk' camera and the four looks are invented; every photo is drawn in your browser, and no camera is used. This is a learning concept demo, not the official Gloam Cam app.

About this demo

A learning concept demo written after a research report; not the official Gloam Cam app. Interface and copy are original — no official logo, icon, screenshots, camera-body names or film names; the 'Dusk' camera, the four looks and the scenes are invented. Publisher noted as the independent developer Yingze Fan. The report found the app launched on 2026-08-09, built word of mouth in mainland China first, and in AppMagic's 24 November data jumped to #2 (+551) on Taiwan's iPhone free chart (games included) and #4 (+348) in Hong Kong; the jump coincided with an Apple feature in Taiwan recorded by a third party, not with a store update. It monetises through an annual plan and a lifetime unlock. This demo does only the re-roll-after-the-shot step, with no payments. Nothing leaves this page; a refresh clears it.
