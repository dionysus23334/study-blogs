Check this draw

Invoice Monster Spring Trees Technology CO., LTD. 2026-11-27

Taiwan's uniform-invoice lottery is drawn on the 25th of every odd-numbered month. The ten-year-old receipt-checker in today's report is usually outside Taiwan's overall top 50 free chart, yet on draw day 25 November 2026 it jumped to #3 on the apps daily chart. Its idea is simple: save the receipts first, and check them when the numbers come out. This demo does just that one step: match a stack of invented practice receipts against an invented draw. The numbers are all made up and cannot be used to check real receipts. This is a learning concept demo, not the official Invoice Monster app.

About this demo

A learning concept demo written after a research report; not the official Invoice Monster app. Interface and copy are original — no official logo, icon, screenshots or characters. The practice receipts, draw numbers and the small geometric stamp are invented. Publisher: Spring Trees Technology CO., LTD. The report found that on AppMagic's lastDate 25 November 2026 (the Ministry of Finance draw for the Sep–Oct 2026 term) the app reached #3 (+855) on Taiwan's iPhone apps free daily chart, with three other checker apps jumping the same day; version 4.20.0 on 20 November only added a custom sticker for the carrier widget, so the jump was not update-triggered. No subscription SKU was found; monetisation is one-off in-app purchases and ads, plus the developer's brand partnerships. This demo does only the 'check this draw' step, with no payments and no real lottery data. Nothing leaves this page; a refresh clears it.
