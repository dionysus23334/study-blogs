About this demo

This is a study sketch made after a research note, not the official WorkBuddy product, and it does not use the official mark. On the China mainland store the app is listed as WorkBuddy - AI 办公我帮你, published by Tencent Technology (Shanghai) Company Limited. The page keeps one idea: a quiet desk, and a short “help me do this.” The meeting, the reply, and the three tasks are invented. Turn the sheets with the buttons or the left and right arrow keys. Checks stay on this page only; a refresh returns the desk to morning.
