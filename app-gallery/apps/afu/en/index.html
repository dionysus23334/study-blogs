Say where it hurts

蚂蚁阿福 Ant Afu Ant Yikang (Guangzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd. 2026-11-24

Many health questions get stuck at step one: you can't say where it hurts or what it feels like. The app in today's report just launched a video 'look at it' consult built around follow-up questions that help you describe a symptom, with a human doctor available to review. This demo opens no camera and makes no judgement — it only shows the follow-up step: pick a start, answer a few questions, and a description card you could show a doctor builds up on the right. The scenarios, questions and the assistant 'Wren' are invented. This is a learning concept demo, not the official Ant Afu product, and not medical advice.
