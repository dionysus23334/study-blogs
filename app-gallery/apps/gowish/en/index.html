About this demo

      
This is a study concept demo made after a research note, not the official GoWish product. The interface and the copy are original, and the page does not use the official mark, screenshots, or brand colors. The publisher is recorded as Onskeskyen ApS / Ønskeskyen ApS. The page keeps one idea: a short list you can add a wish to, then leave one reserved for someone else. The gloves, the cookbook, the ticket, and anything you write are invented. A reservation stays on this page only; a refresh returns the first three. Passing the list only copies a note onto this device. Nothing is sent.
