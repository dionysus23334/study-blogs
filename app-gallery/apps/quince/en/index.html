Sale day

Quince Lastbrand Inc. 2026-11-23

A well-made thing has several layers in its price: materials, making, shipping, and the markups along the way. This demo first takes one item's price apart; then it puts you in a 'sale day' — a 24-hour early sale that happens only a few times a year, where the lower price only applies when you check out in the app. Drag the clock to see when it starts, how the web and the app differ, and how the stock runs down. The items, shop, costs and stock are invented. This is a learning concept demo, not the official Quince product.

      About this demo

      A learning concept demo written after a research report; not an official Quince product. Interface and copy are original — no official logo, icon, screenshots, product photos or brand colours; the shop name 'Plainthread' is invented. Publisher noted as Lastbrand Inc. The report found that the app's spikes on the US iPhone charts (2026-03-25, 10-06, 11-20) each lined up with a 24-hour sale that only works at checkout in the app, not with a version update — and some reviews complain about being pushed to install the app for a discount. So the demo puts the two together: where the price comes from, and why that one day happens in the app. Nothing leaves this page; a refresh clears it.
